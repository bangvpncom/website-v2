Delete Your BangVPN Account

Last Updated: October 2026

You can delete your BangVPN account at any time. There are two ways to do it: directly in the BangVPN app, or by emailing our support team.

Before You Delete: Cancel Your Subscription

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel a paid subscription. If you subscribed through the Apple App Store or Google Play, your subscription is billed by Apple or Google, and you must cancel it there to stop future charges.

  • iPhone or iPad: Open Settings, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions and select BangVPN.
  • Android: Open the Google Play app, tap your profile icon, then tap Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions and select BangVPN.

Refunds are handled as described in our Terms of Service and by the store where you made your purchase.

Option 1: Delete Your Account in the App

  1. Open the BangVPN app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account, next to your email address.
  4. Confirm that you want to permanently delete your account.

Option 2: Request Deletion by Email

If you can't access the app, you can ask us to delete your account for you:

  1. Send an email to support@bangvpn.com with the subject “Delete my BangVPN account.”
  2. Send it from the email address linked to your BangVPN account, if possible.
  3. We will verify your identity to make sure the request comes from the account owner.
  4. Once verified, we will delete your account and confirm by email.

What Happens When You Delete Your Account

  • Your account and its associated personal information, such as your email address and login credentials, are permanently deleted.
  • You will be signed out of BangVPN on all of your devices and will no longer be able to connect to the VPN with that account.
  • Account deletion cannot be undone. To use BangVPN again, you will need to create a new account.
  • Because of our no-logs policy, we do not store your browsing activity, so there is no browsing history to delete.

We may retain limited information where required by law, for example billing records needed for tax or accounting purposes. See our Privacy Policy for more information about the data we collect and how long we keep it.

Questions?

If you need help deleting your account, contact us at support@bangvpn.com.
